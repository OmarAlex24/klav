# Wordlist for passphrases

`eff_large_wordlist.txt` is the *EFF Large Wordlist for Passphrases* (7776 words), from the Electronic Frontier Foundation, published at <https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases> and downloaded unchanged from <https://www.eff.org/files/2016/07/18/eff_large_wordlist.txt> (SHA-256 `addd35536511597a02fa0a9ff1e5284677b8883b83e986e43f15a3db996b903e`).
