This section takes you from nothing to a working vault. Read its pages in this order:

1. [Install Klav](/get-started/install/): which parts of Klav you can get today, what each one needs, and how to set it up.
2. [Create an account](/get-started/create-an-account/): on Klav's server or on your own, and the emergency kit you keep afterwards.
3. [Import your passwords](/get-started/import/): from Bitwarden, 1Password, Google Authenticator or a browser.
4. [Add a device](/get-started/add-a-device/): sign in on your other computers and your iPhone.
