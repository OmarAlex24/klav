This page explains how to start using Klav on another device: another Mac, a Linux computer or your iPhone. You sign in there with your email, your master password and the Secret Key from your [emergency kit](/get-started/create-an-account/#the-emergency-kit). The new device downloads your vaults and works like the first one.

## What you need

- **Your emergency kit.** It has your Secret Key, and the server your account lives on (its `Server:` line). If you can't find it, see it again on a device that is already signed in: `klav account kit` in the CLI, or Settings › Account › "Print the emergency kit" on the iPhone.
- **Your master password.**
- **Klav on the new device.** See [Install Klav](/get-started/install/).

If your account lives on your own server, give its address on the new device too: "Use my own server" in the apps, `--server` in the CLI. The default is klav.dev.

## On a Mac, with the Mac app

<Steps>

1. In "Set up this Mac", go to the Account step and choose "Sign in".
2. "Sign in on this Mac" asks for your email, your master password, your Secret Key and a name for this Mac. For your own server, choose "Use my own server" and type its address.
3. Choose "Sign in".

</Steps>

The master password and the Secret Key stay on this Mac. The extension and the CLI on this Mac use the same account.

## On the iPhone

<Steps>

1. Open Klav. "Sign in on this iPhone" asks for your email, your master password, your Secret Key and a name for this iPhone.
2. For your own server, choose "Use my own server" and type its address.
3. Choose "Sign in".
4. Turn on AutoFill when the app shows you how: in the iPhone's Settings, open General, then AutoFill & Passwords, and turn on Klav.

</Steps>

## With the CLI

On macOS or Linux, give `klav login` your email and the kit file. It reads only the Secret Key from the file:

```sh
klav login --email you@example.com --emergency-kit klav-kit.txt
```

```text
Server: https://api.klav.dev (klav.dev). Use --server URL for your own.
Master password:
Deriving keys (Argon2id, 256 MiB)…
Logged in as you@example.com on device your-desktop (…). 1 vault(s) synced.
```

Without `--emergency-kit`, it asks for the Secret Key first, without showing what you type: `Secret Key (K1-…): `. For your own server, add `--server https://klav.example.com`. The device name is the computer's host name unless you pass `--device-name`.

Check that the device is signed in. `klav status` needs no password:

```sh
klav status
```

```text
home:      /Users/you/Library/Application Support/klav
server:    https://api.klav.dev
account:   you@example.com (…)
device:    your-desktop (…)
words:     …
daemon:    not running
```

Then `klav unlock` starts `klavd`, so commands stop asking for the password. On a Mac, after the first unlock with your password, `klavd` asks for Touch ID instead.

## Removing a device

No app lists or removes your devices yet. To stop using Klav on a device, sign out on it: `klav logout` closes its session on the server and deletes its copy of the account. Your account stays on the server.
