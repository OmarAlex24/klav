This section explains how Klav protects your data, what it defends against and what it doesn't, and how to report a vulnerability. Its pages summarize Klav's [cryptography specification](https://github.com/OmarAlex24/klav/blob/main/docs/especificacion-cripto.md) and [threat model](https://github.com/OmarAlex24/klav/blob/main/docs/modelo-de-amenazas.md), which are the source of truth: where a page here and those documents differ, they win.

1. [How Klav protects your data](/security/how-klav-protects-your-data/): end-to-end encryption, your master password and Secret Key, signing in with OPAQUE, the keys inside your account, your devices, and what the server sees.
2. [What Klav defends against](/security/what-klav-defends-against/): a stolen database, a dishonest server, a stolen device, agents and prompt injection, phishing, and what is out of scope.
3. [Report a vulnerability](/security/report-a-vulnerability/): how to report one privately and what happens next.

<Aside type="caution" title="Early development">
Klav is in early development. Don't use it for real secrets yet. It hasn't had an external security audit; one is planned before version 1.0, and its report will be published.
</Aside>
